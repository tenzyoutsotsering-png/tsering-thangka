01 — The tradition

A painting is more than an image.

Thangka painting brings together sacred proportion, drawing, colour, shading, fine detail and gold work. It is a discipline learned through observation and practice, one careful line at a time.

The studio keeps the learning intimate: small groups, close guidance and time spent actually making.

03 — The studio

Not a museum replica. A living practice.

The studio is a small, focused atelier in McLeod Ganj. Students work at the same tables, with the same patience and attention to detail that define the craft.

Workshops are deliberately limited. Materials are provided. The emphasis is on individual attention, quality and depth rather than rushing through a souvenir experience.
