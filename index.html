01 — The tradition

Where the line becomes a language.

Thangka painting brings together sacred proportion, drawing, natural pigments, shading, fine detail and gold work. It is learned slowly, through observation and practice.

The studio keeps the learning intimate: small groups, close guidance and time spent actually making.

03 — The studio

A living practice, still being passed on.

Tsering Thangka Studio is a small, focused atelier in McLeod Ganj, Dharamsala, led by Bhuchung Tsering, with more than 40 years of experience.

Workshops are deliberately limited. Materials are provided. The emphasis is on individual attention, quality and depth rather than rushing through a souvenir experience.
