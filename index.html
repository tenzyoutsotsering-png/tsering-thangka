01 · The tradition

A painting is more than an image.

Thangka painting is a disciplined visual practice. Proportion, line, colour and detail are not added at random. They are learned through careful observation and repeated practice. At Tsering Thangka Studio, students learn these foundations directly, at their own pace, in a close teacher-student setting.

01 · ONE DAY · ₹3,000

First Lines

A gentle introduction to Thangka painting for beginners. Learn simple elements such as flowers, clouds, plants or a Buddha head, then paint a small piece on a provided canvas to take home.

10 AM–4 PM · Maximum 10 students · Materials provided.

02 · TWO DAYS · ₹4,500

Form & Proportion

Continue beyond the basics with an introduction to Thangka iconometry, including the proportions of the figure, while developing more of the drawing and painting process.

10 AM–4 PM · Maximum 10 students · Materials provided.

03 · THREE DAYS · ₹6,000

Depth & Detail

Three days give more time to develop the painting, adding greater depth, detail and refinement while building on the foundations of traditional Thangka practice.

10 AM–4 PM · Maximum 10 students · Materials provided.

04 · ONE MONTH · ₹20,000

Complete Training

A deeper studio practice, five days a week from 10 AM–4 PM, with a maximum of three students. Study iconometry, pigments and colour, shading, fine detail and gold work. Depending on progress, students may complete a simple Thangka.

Start dates are arranged according to studio availability. Accommodation is not provided, but the studio can help students find a nearby place to stay.

05 · The studio

Small by design.
Deep by practice.

Tsering Thangka Studio is a small, cozy working atelier in McLeod Ganj, Dharamsala. The studio keeps classes deliberately limited so students can work closely with the teacher and receive individual attention.

Master Bhuchung Tsering has more than 40 years of experience in Thangka painting and has exhibited his work in India and several European countries. Master students may assist with teaching when appropriate.

There is no need to arrive with prior Thangka knowledge. For the short workshops, basic drawing skills are helpful. Materials are provided.

06 · Commission a Thangka

Made slowly.
Made for you.

Commission a traditional Thangka created with mineral and natural pigments, with gold work where appropriate. You can choose the subject and discuss custom compositions or details within traditional iconographic rules.

A typical commission takes around six months. The process is simple and personal: request, discussion, quotation, 30% deposit, sketch, painting, completion, remaining payment and shipping. Progress updates are shared throughout the painting process.

Commissions can be shipped within India or internationally, and framing is available.
