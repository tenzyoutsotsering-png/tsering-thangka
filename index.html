01 · The tradition

A painting is more than an image.

Thangka painting is a disciplined visual language. Every line, proportion, colour and detail belongs to a tradition carried from teacher to student. At Tsering Thangka Studio, the emphasis is on learning that discipline slowly, carefully and in person.

05 · The studio

Small by design.
Deep by practice.

Tsering Thangka Studio is a small, cozy working atelier in McLeod Ganj, Dharamsala. Classes are deliberately limited so students can receive individual attention rather than disappear into a crowded room.

Master Bhuchung Tsering brings more than four decades of experience in Thangka painting, with exhibitions in India and Europe. Master students may assist with teaching when appropriate.

Commission a traditional Thangka painted with mineral and natural pigments and optional gold work. Custom compositions and details can be discussed within traditional iconographic rules.
