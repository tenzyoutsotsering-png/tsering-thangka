01 — The tradition

A painting is more than an image.

Thangka painting brings together sacred proportion, drawing, colour, shading, fine detail and gold work. At Tsering Thangka Studio, these disciplines are taught through close, practical guidance.

A small, focused atelier where students learn by drawing, observing and practicing.
