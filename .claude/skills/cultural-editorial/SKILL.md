# Cultural Editorial Skill

Use this skill when writing or rewriting copy for Tsering Thangka Studio.

## Goal
Create copy that feels like a real Tibetan Thangka atelier speaking: knowledgeable, warm, restrained, human, and culturally grounded. Avoid generic tourism language, exaggerated spirituality, and corporate marketing clichés.

## Voice
- Warm, precise, confident, and quietly premium.
- Contemporary editorial language with respect for traditional practice.
- Prefer concrete descriptions of drawing, proportion, pigments, shading, detail, gold work, teaching, and studio practice.
- Let the artwork and studio experience carry the emotion instead of overclaiming.
- Use short, memorable headlines and informative supporting text.

## Cultural accuracy
- Never invent lineage, affiliations, teachers, credentials, history, or religious claims.
- Do not imply affiliation with the Institute of Tibetan Thangka Art unless explicitly verified.
- Preserve Tibetan terms when they add meaning, and explain them where needed.
- Avoid treating Tibetan culture as an exotic aesthetic.

## Content structure
For each section, establish:
1. A clear human benefit or idea.
2. Specific evidence or description.
3. A useful action such as Explore, Enquire, Apply, or Request a Commission.

## Avoid
- "Discover the sacred world..."
- "Ancient secrets..."
- Empty claims such as "masterpiece" or "timeless beauty" without evidence.
- Excessive exclamation marks and mystical language.
- Repetition of "traditional Tibetan art" in every section.
