# SEO Content Skill

Use this skill to improve discoverability without sacrificing the studio's editorial voice.

## Focus
- Use natural phrases relevant to the actual business: Thangka painting classes, Thangka workshops in McLeod Ganj, Dharamsala, Tibetan Thangka painting, Thangka commissions, and Tibetan art training.
- Write descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
- Use clear H1/H2 hierarchy.
- Include location information naturally where useful.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and generic AI-generated tourism copy.
- Keep claims factual and limited to information verified by the studio.
